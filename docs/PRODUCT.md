# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who use an iPhone or iPad to search, organize, and play music and podcasts from supported online services or a local library.

## Product Purpose

Mono is a SwiftUI music and podcast player. It should make discovery, search, library management, playback, and settings feel direct and dependable without distracting from the media.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, restrained. The interface should feel mature and deliberate while preserving recognizable album art, podcast artwork, icons, and core media content.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative gradients, textures, illustrations, stacked cards, ornamental labels, marketing copy, duplicated information, and explanatory filler. Minimalism must not remove necessary controls, states, or information.

## Design Principles

- Keep the media and the current task visually dominant.
- Establish hierarchy through type, spacing, alignment, and subtle separation.
- Use one consistent component vocabulary across every screen and state.
- Keep visible copy functional, concise, and non-repetitive.
- Preserve complete workflows while removing decoration that does not serve them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve native accessibility semantics, Dynamic Type behavior, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and non-color-only state cues across supported iPhone and iPad layouts.
